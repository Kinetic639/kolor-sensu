## Active asset prompt

Create a new ultra-wide 3:1 website footer texture. The surface is pale warm blush pink #F6DCDD, softly hand painted in matte gouache with very subtle fine broad horizontal brush strokes and LOW RELIEF, calm and uniform enough to put small dark navigation links over it. Entire canvas is OPAQUE, NO TRANSPARENCY. Only the top approximately 4% is the flat warm off-white page-paper color #F8F7F3. The boundary where pale blush paint meets this warm off-white is visibly formed by ONE BROAD FLAT BRUSH DRAGGED SIDEWAYS from left to right: coherent, overlapping horizontal slightly tapered creamy brush strokes, with a few delicate horizontal bristle trails CONNECTED to the painted area and gently irregular shallow contours. The boundary's height variation is very small and never reaches below 7% of the image height. It must look like the clean edge of a loaded paintbrush, NOT airbrush spray, NOT tiny dots, NOT foam, NOT torn paper, NOT granular distressing. There are NO isolated splashes, speckles, dots or islands above the pink paint. The off-white region above is perfectly clean and plain. Below the shallow top edge, the pink paint fills everything to the left, right and bottom edge with no margins or exposed paper on those three sides, no brush-end contours down there. Preserve pleasant light blush pink with very quiet shallow painterly texture: no gray cast, no green, no strong dark patches, no thick impasto ridges. No digital fade or opacity gradient at the boundary. No words, no objects, no leaves or branches, no frames. It is a full-bleed pale pink painted footer surface with only a tiny coherent horizontally brushed top edge.

## Integration

A nine-slice border image keeps the top brush strokes shallow regardless of footer height. Side and bottom slices are zero; the opaque center fills the footer to its edges. No CSS gradient or opacity fade is used.
